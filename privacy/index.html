Privacy Policy

We keep it simple. No tracking, no ads, no funny business. Last updated June 13, 2026.

TL;DR

We don't track you. We don't have accounts, cookies, or analytics. This is a static site hosted on Cloudflare. We don't sell anything yet — but if we do, we'll tell you what changes. That's pretty much it.

1. What We Collect

Nothing. This is a static information site — no accounts, no logins, no comment sections, no forms, no cookies we set ourselves. We don't run analytics scripts. We don't track individual visitors.

2. Third-Party Services

  • Cloudflare — Hosts and delivers the site. Cloudflare processes technical data (IP address, browser type) as part of serving pages and DDoS protection. Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — We load fonts from Google Fonts. Google may log the request. We don't use Google Analytics or any other Google tracking. Google's privacy policy.

3. How We Use Information

We don't collect any, so there's nothing to use. If that changes (e.g., we add an email signup or sell a guide), we'll update this page and tell you what changed.

4. Data Retention

We don't store anything. Cloudflare retains access logs per their own policy for operational purposes.

5. Your Rights

Since we don't hold any personal data, there's nothing to access, correct, or delete. If you have questions anyway, email us.

6. Contact

Privacy questions? privacy@installsafe.dev.
